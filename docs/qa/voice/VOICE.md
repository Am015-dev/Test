# C10 — the voice pass

Every user-facing sentence in scope, rewritten as an experienced Athens estate agent would say it to a 65-year-old buyer: one idea per sentence, the number first, the reason second, no statistics words («διάμεσος», «δείγμα», «εκτίμηση από ζητούμενα»), no English, no hedges, no «το σύστημα», no apologies. Every number and its basis is kept, and so are the glossary nouns for the five card lines.

## Blind read (isolated sub-agent, ten random after-sentences, «sounds like a person» /10)

Each round used a fresh agent that saw only the ten sentences, rendered from the live DB (`data/listings.db`) by calling the real functions, and a new random sample.

**The ≥ 8 bar is not met.** What the readers still mark down is mostly required by the brief or owned elsewhere: basis counts in brackets (the brief says to keep every number's basis), the glossary's «~» and «Προσφορά:» lines, the Greek «54 %» spacing, «Για ανατίμηση:» headings, the market_intel comparables label and the informal register. Closing the gap needs one of these decisions from the coordinator: (a) move basis counts behind «Από πού βγαίνουν» instead of inline, (b) switch the app to the polite plural, (c) give the comparables label a spoken form in `market_intel`.
